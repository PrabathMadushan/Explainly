⭐ Behavioral Story – Innovation Nation (IEEE)
Story Title

Eliminating Hosting Costs Through an Offline-First Architecture

S – Situation

I worked on the Innovation Nation platform, a startup-focused project conducted under IEEE.
The project had very limited funding, and during early discussions the team considered using EC2 or VPS hosting, which was too expensive for the scale of the platform.

T – Task

My responsibility was to propose a cost-effective hosting solution that could reliably support the platform without creating ongoing infrastructure costs.

A – Action

I suggested moving away from traditional servers and using Firebase instead.
However, I identified a key risk: Firestore read and write costs could still become expensive if not designed carefully.

To solve this, I:

Designed an offline-first architecture

Enabled Firestore persistence, so most data is stored and read locally

Structured the application to sync only when actual data changes

Avoided unnecessary real-time listeners to minimize reads and writes

Optimized the data model based on real usage patterns

Since the platform had around 50 teams with a maximum of 10 members per team, this approach was more than sufficient without over-engineering.

R – Result

As a result:

Hosting cost was reduced by 100%

The entire platform ran on Firebase Free Tier

There were no performance or data consistency issues

The solution fully met the project’s requirements within budget

This experience taught me how architecture decisions directly affect cost, and how designing around actual usage can completely eliminate unnecessary infrastructure expenses.
